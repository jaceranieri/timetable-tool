# Primary Timetable Helper Tool: FAQs

## What’s new

- **A simpler setup wizard.** Five steps: Core Schedule, Classes, Specialists, Activities and Summary. Each one is checked as you go.
- **Your progress is saved.** Come back later and pick up where you left off, or save your setup to a file.
- **Change your timetable after it’s made.** Add, rename or remove classes and specialists without starting again.
- **Start next year from this year.** Reuse last year’s setup as the starting point for a new timetable.
- **A Start here tab** in every timetable, with the steps to follow and a list of documents you’ve made.
- **Built-in checks** that tell you which classes are compliant and what needs fixing.
- **Copy tools** that fill the rest of the year from your first week or first term.
- **Google Docs of your timetables**, ready to share with staff or print.

## About the tool

**Do I have to use the Primary Timetable Helper Tool for all my class timetables?**
No, the tool is optional. It’s simply designed to help schools with the complex task of timetabling for compliance.

**Can I use the tool for just one class?**
Yes. The wizard starts with two Kindergarten classes and every other grade empty. Remove the classes you don’t need, add the one you do with the **+** button, and the tool will only generate a timetable for that class.

**Why do I need to timetable the whole year for compliance?**
Compliance is based on the percentage of teaching time across the entire calendar year, so you need to complete timetables for the whole year to calculate this accurately.

**Can I use this sheet as evidence for an audit?**
Yes, as long as all relevant classes have completed timetables in the sheet, you can use it for audit purposes. **Timetable Admin > Make a Google Doc of the compliance summary** gives you a one-page summary of every class to keep with your records.

**I have a composite classroom of 2 grades in 1 classroom, can I combine them into 1 timetable?**
Unfortunately, from a compliance perspective, you will need to have separate compliant timetables for each grade’s students.

## Setting up with the wizard

**What happens the first time I open the wizard?**
Google will ask you to allow the tool to access your Drive and Sheets. This is needed so it can create your timetable in your own Google Drive. Your timetable is saved in a **Timetables** folder in My Drive.

**Do I have to finish the setup in one go?**
No. Your progress is saved as you work, and the wizard offers to continue where you left off the next time you open it. You can also use **☰ > Save setup to a file** to keep a copy, and **☰ > Load a saved setup** to bring it back.

**Can I paste my class list from a spreadsheet instead of typing it?**
Yes. Open the **⋯** menu on a grade and choose **Paste from a spreadsheet**. Copy two columns from your spreadsheet (class name, then teacher) and paste them in.

**Why can’t I leave the teacher’s name blank or enter TBC?**
Every class timetable needs a named teacher for compliance. If you’re still waiting on staffing, enter the name of the teacher most likely to take the class and update it once it’s confirmed.

**My classes run on a fortnightly cycle. Can the tool handle that?**
Yes. Each grade and each specialist can have its own cycle, from 1 to 10 weeks. Set it with the cycle button next to the grade or specialist.

**Do I need to set up Amadeus?**
No. Amadeus Tutorials are added automatically for Years 3–6, and the compliance check includes them for those classes.

## Working in your timetable

**Where do I start once my timetable is made?**
Open the **Start here** tab. It walks you through each step in order, shows when specialists were last synced and lists every Google Doc you’ve made from the timetable.

**How do specialist lessons get into the class timetables?**
Enter each specialist’s lessons on their own tab, then choose **Timetable Admin > 1. Sync Specialist Schedules**. The lessons are copied into the class timetables for you. **2. Lock Synced Cells** stops them being changed by accident. If two specialists are booked with the same class at the same time, the slot shows in red.

**Do I have to fill in every week by hand?**
No. Fill in your first week, then use **Timetable Admin > Copy Week A into the other weeks** (for timetables that run on a cycle) or **Copy Term 1 into Terms 2-4**. You choose whether to fill only the empty slots or replace what’s already there. Breaks and specialist lessons are never changed.

**How do I check whether a timetable is compliant?**
On a class tab, choose **Timetable Admin > Check this timetable**. It shows which KLAs are under or over their range and how many slots are still empty. **Check all class timetables** does the same for every class at once. The **OVERVIEW** tab also shows each KLA in green or red for every class.

**Can I share or print a timetable?**
Yes. On a class or specialist tab, choose **Timetable Admin > Make a Google Doc of this timetable**. Pick a single week or **All terms**. The document is coloured like your timetable and saved in the same folder. It is also listed on the Start here tab.

**Can I add more detail to each timetable block using this tool?**
No, the tool is designed to allocate compliant KLA and Additional Hours for teaching time. If you want to add further detail (e.g. specifying Dance or Visual Arts within Creative Arts), make a Google Doc of the timetable and add those details there. This extra detail isn’t required for NESA compliance.

## Making changes

**Am I locked into my timetable once I set it?**
No, you can update your timetable as needed, as long as you maintain the required ratios over the year. Use **Check this timetable** to see how changes affect compliance for each class.

**I need to add a class or a specialist after my timetable is made. Do I have to start again?**
No. In the wizard, choose **☰ > Edit an existing timetable** and paste the link to your timetable. You can then add, rename or remove classes and specialists.
- New ones get their own tabs.
- Renamed ones keep their lessons.
- Removed ones are hidden, not deleted, in case you need them again.

You’ll need edit access to the timetable.

**Can I change the bell times or breaks once my timetable is made?**
Not in an existing timetable, because every lesson is placed against those times. Choose **☰ > Start from an existing timetable** in the wizard to make a new timetable with the same setup, then change the times before you generate it.

**Can I reuse this year’s setup for next year?**
Yes. Choose **☰ > Start from an existing timetable** in the wizard and paste the link to this year’s timetable. Your classes, specialists and times are filled in, ready to update for the new year.

## Compliance questions

**Why isn’t the English minimum always 30%?**
English and Mathematics are checked together: combined, they need at least 50% of teaching time, with Mathematics at least 20%. The English minimum adjusts to match. For example, a class with 23% Mathematics needs at least 27% English. Religion’s minimum depends on the grade: 10% for K–2 and 12% for Years 3–6.

## Troubleshooting

**My sheet isn’t calculating ratios anymore. What should I do?**
It’s likely some formulas were changed or deleted. The best solution is to revert to a previous version where everything was working (**File > Version history**) or make a new copy of the tool.

**I get an error saying “a server error occurred while reading from storage” when I use the Timetable Admin menu.**
This happens when you’re signed into more than one Google account in your browser. Timetables made with the latest version of the tool don’t have this problem. If yours is older, open it in a Chrome window signed into only your school account, or ask for it to be updated.
